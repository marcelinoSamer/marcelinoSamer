### `>` whoami

I work at the point where physics stops being abstract and starts being an optimization problem you actually have to solve. My research maps real-world combinatorial problems — currently multi-target RF-based indoor localization — onto QUBO formulations, and studies how quantum annealers and QAOA find their way to the bottom of the energy landscape compared to classical simulated annealing.

The rest of the time I write TypeScript for a living: backend systems, APIs, and full-stack products for four different clients, ranging from social feed infrastructure to design-heavy client platforms. Same instinct either way — reduce the search space, kill the local minima, ship something that converges.

```
  energy
    │  ╭─╮                         ╭──╮
    │  │ ╰╮      ╭─╮      ╭──╮    ╭╯  │
    │  │  ╰╮    ╭╯ ╰╮    ╭╯  ╰────╯   │
    │  │   ╰────╯   ╰────╯            ╰── ground state (hopefully)
    └──┴──────────────────────────────────────▶ configuration space
       classical gets stuck here   quantum tunnels through
```

<br>

### `>` currently annealing on

- 🧲 **QUBO formulations for multi-target RF localization** — matroid structure for AP selection, benchmarked against simulated quantum annealing (SQA)
- 🔁 **QAOA vs. quantum annealing** — where variational gate-based approaches hold up against dedicated annealing hardware, and where they don't
- 🛠️ **A dev tool for local database visualization + AI-assisted codebase Q&A** — dogfooding it against my own client backends
- 📽️ Building science-communication content on quantum computing — because the gap between "quantum is cool" and "quantum is *this specific mechanism*" is where most people get lost, and I'd rather close it than exploit it
